how it will work

From the dungeon to your PayPal in four steps.

Get $

Pick up at least tokens. That's the smallest bag that gets a hero.

02

Lock your bag

Your tokens go into the vault and a hero is created for you. You can take them back whenever you like.

03

Win the hour

Cash out

Send your winnings to PayPal as dollars, or keep them in SOL. Your choice, every time.

questions

Cash-out FAQ.

Can I cash out to PayPal today?

Not yet. Cash-out is in development and this page shows the design. Today the game runs as a demo; winnings will be paid in SOL first, with PayPal coming after.

Do I need a PayPal account?

Yes, a PayPal account in a country where PayPal can receive payouts. You'll also be asked to verify your identity before your first cash-out, as payment providers require.

What will it cost?

The exchange rate and every fee will be shown on the review screen before you confirm. Nothing is taken until you press the button.

Do I have to leave the dungeon to cash out?

No. Cashing out only touches your winnings. Your bag stays locked and your hero keeps falling every hour.

Can I keep my winnings in SOL instead?

Always. PayPal is an option, not a requirement.
