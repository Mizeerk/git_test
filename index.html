    How each hour is decided.

    1. 01

      Bag size. Your chance is your bag multiplied by its time bonus, divided by the same number for everyone together. Spreading one bag over several wallets gains you nothing.

    2. 02

      Staying power. The bonus climbs from ×1 to ×2 across days in the vault. Topping up keeps the token-time you already earned, so a larger bag restarts a little lower.

    3. 03

      Fair randomness. Each hour's seed is the hash of the next Solana slot once the hour closes. Nobody can see it ahead of time, and every hero's landing floor follows from it.

    % of the coin's creator fees go into the pots and % to the dev. If an hour has no winner, its pot carries over to the next one.
