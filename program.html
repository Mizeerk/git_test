program

Every instruction, one by one.

join(amount)you

Transfers tokens from your wallet to the vault and creates your hero or grows it. The bag is whatever actually arrived.

leaveyou

Returns your whole bag and closes the hero. Allowed at any time.

syncanyone

Books newly arrived fees: % to the dev, % to the pot.

sealanyone

After an hour's last slot: reads the seed from SlotHashes, freezes the pot and starts the next hour.

descend(heroes…)anyone

During the window after a seal: computes each qualifying hero's key and records the deepest.

settleanyone

After the window: sends the frozen pot to the deepest hero's owner, or carries it forward.

claim_devanyone

Sends the booked dev share to the dev wallet.

init · set_mint · set_dev · renounce_admindeploy key

Creates the game, sets the coin once (rejected if the mint has freeze, tax or clawback powers), moves the dev share, and renounces the key.

The key.

Each hero draws an exponential with rate equal to its weight; the smallest draw lands deepest. On chain this is done in fixed-point integers. The demo on this site uses the same formula in floating point.

// u in (0, 1] from the hash; -log2(u) is an exponential draw
u      = (first bytes of sha256(seed || owner) + 1) / 2^bits
e      = -log2(u)
bonus  = 1 + min(age,  days) /  days
weight = bag * bonus
// smaller key = deeper floor
key    = e / weight
P(win) = weight / sum(weight of every hero in the hour)

Because the seed is public once the hour is sealed, anyone can recompute every hero's key and check the order themselves.
